### B.Com General

### B.Com Bank Management
- Department established in 2018.
- Blends commerce, banking, finance and technology.
- Includes industry experts, training sessions, and guidance for professional qualifications such as CFA, CMA, MBA and other finance certifications.
- Strong emphasis on ethics, leadership and communication skills.
- Vision: to be a best-in-class department, in collaboration with industry, providing quality education in banking and finance.

### B.Sc Visual Communication
- 3-year programme in visual media and communication.
- Covers graphic design, photography, film studies, animation and media production.
- Students learn to tell stories through pictures and videos.
- Includes internships, workshops and industry collaborations.
- Careers: graphic design, photography, film production, animation, media planning, advertising.
- Department association: "Artitects" (fosters creativity, innovation and professional growth).
- Mission: to give students the skills, knowledge and tools to explore, create and communicate through visual media.

### BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)
- 3-year programme; the department was established in 2019.
- Covers programming languages, database management, software engineering and web technologies.
- Experiential learning through internships, workshops and industry collaborations.
- Final-year students do internships in reputed companies, and students work on real-time projects in e-commerce and web development.
- Workshops held on IoT, MongoDB, Ethical Hacking and AI. Seminars held on Data Analytics, Blockchain, Cloud Computing and Cyber Security. Webinars held on Cyber Security, Low Code/No Code, AI and ML.
- MoUs with Pione Technology (Chennai) and DOQFY (Bangalore). The department is a partner of ICT Academy, which provides faculty development programs and seminars.
- Students have been placed in IT companies including Infosys, TCS, CTS, HCLTech, Accenture, Ntrust Infotech, Sutherland, HDFC Bank and Eyetex. Some students pursue higher studies in India and abroad.
- Careers: software development, database administration, system analysis, web development, IT management.
- Vision: to emerge as a center of excellence in computer applications by nurturing innovation, research and industry-ready skills.
